Academic Background & Focus

I am currently a Class 12 Humanities student at Saint Paul's School, Jalpaiguri, West Bengal. My academic pursuit centers on understanding the structural mechanisms of Indian constitutional law, the protection of civil liberties, and the sociological dimensions of crime and justice.

Alongside formal coursework, I conduct independent academic inquiry into how legal systems adapt to modern challenges. A core area of my interest is legal technology: examining how digital access, automated research tools, and responsible technology can improve the reach of legal information without compromising judicial deliberation and accountability.

My objective is to pursue an undergraduate legal education (LL.B.) and build a career combining legal scholarship, constitutional research, and practical advocacy.
